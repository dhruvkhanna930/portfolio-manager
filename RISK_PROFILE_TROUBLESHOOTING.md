# Risk Profile Form - Troubleshooting Guide

## Issue: Can't Submit Risk Profile Questionnaire

### ✅ Solution: Make Sure All Questions Are Answered

**Problem**: The form won't move forward or submit when you click buttons.

**Why This Happens**: You must select ONE answer for EVERY question (Q1-Q13) before submitting.

### Step-by-Step Fix

1. **Complete the Form**
   - Start from Question 1
   - For each question, click ONE radio button option
   - Click "Next" to proceed to the next question

2. **What Question 13 Looks Like**
   ```
   Question 13/13: "You invest ₹ 1,00,000 in a share that goes down by 8% 
   the next day. You will..."
   
   Select ONE option:
   ☐ average your cost by investing another ₹ 1,00,000 at a lower price
   ☐ do not bother because you had done enough research on the company
   ☐ book your loss and invest in fixed deposits or bonds
   ☐ hold on till the share comes back to your cost price and sell it
   ```

3. **Click Submit**
   - After answering Question 13, click the blue **"Submit"** button
   - This will process your answers and show recommendations

### Common Issues

#### Issue 1: Submit Button Doesn't Appear
**Solution**: Scroll down or make sure you're on Question 13 (the page should say "13/13")

#### Issue 2: Button Appears but Nothing Happens
**Solution**: Make sure you have selected ONE of the four options for Question 13 by clicking the label/circle

#### Issue 3: Error Message About Missing Answers
**Solution**: Go back through previous questions and make sure each question has exactly ONE option selected
- Use the "Previous" button to navigate back
- Click on each question's option
- Then click "Next" to continue

### How to Navigate the Form

**Moving Forward**:
1. Select one radio button option
2. Click "Next" button (blue button at bottom)
3. Form moves to next question

**Moving Backward**:
1. Click "Previous" link at top
2. You return to previous question with your answer still selected

**Selecting an Answer**:
- Click directly on the text/label of the option (or the circle)
- Selected option will be highlighted in blue

### What Happens After Submission

1. System calculates your risk profile (Conservative, Balanced, Assertive, or Aggressive)
2. Page redirects to show **10 personalized stock recommendations**
3. You can then add recommended stocks to your portfolio

## Questions About Your Answers?

### Q13 Explanation - Investment Loss Scenario

**Scenario**: You invest ₹100,000 and it drops 8% the next day.

**What Each Option Means**:

1. **"average your cost..."** (Aggressive/Assertive)
   - You believe in the company
   - You buy more at lower price
   - Average down your cost basis
   - For: Investors confident in their research, have extra capital

2. **"do not bother..."** (Assertive)
   - You did good research beforehand
   - Market fluctuation doesn't shake you
   - You hold your position
   - For: Confident, experienced investors

3. **"book your loss..."** (Conservative)
   - You protect capital first
   - 8% drop signals shift to safer investments
   - Move to fixed deposits/bonds
   - For: Risk-averse, capital preservation focused

4. **"hold on till..."** (Balanced)
   - You hold until you break even
   - You want to recover losses but aren't aggressive
   - Patience-based strategy
   - For: Moderate investors with long horizons

### Choose Based On

- Your actual behavior in market downturns
- Your comfort with losses
- Your investment experience
- Your financial situation
